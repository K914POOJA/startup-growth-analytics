# Startup Growth Analytics

## Project Overview
This beginner-level portfolio project uses Python to compare marketing performance across three channels: Instagram, Google Ads, and Email Marketing.

The goal is to understand which channel generates leads and customers more cost-effectively using basic growth metrics.

## Project Objectives
- Compare the performance of three marketing channels.
- Calculate conversion rates.
- Calculate Cost Per Lead (CPL).
- Calculate Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC).
- Develop data-informed marketing recommendations.

## Tools Used
- Python
- Google Colab
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- GitHub

## Dataset
The project uses a fictional practice dataset with marketing spend, leads, and customers for three channels.

| Channel | Spend | Leads | Customers |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Instagram | ₹5,000 | 200 | 20 |
| Google Ads | ₹8,000 | 160 | 24 |
| Email Marketing | ₹2,000 | 150 | 30 |

## Key Metrics
- **Conversion Rate:** Customers ÷ Leads × 100
- **Cost Per Lead (CPL):** Marketing Spend ÷ Leads
- **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC):** Marketing Spend ÷ Customers

## Key Findings
Based on the fictional sample dataset:
- Email Marketing has the highest conversion rate at 20%.
- Email Marketing has the lowest CAC at approximately ₹66.67 per customer.
- Google Ads has the highest CAC at approximately ₹333.33 per customer.
- Instagram has a CAC of ₹250 per customer.

## Recommendation
Email Marketing appears to be the most cost-effective channel in this sample. A real business should validate this finding with actual campaign data and consider revenue, profit, and customer quality before changing its marketing budget.

## Limitations
This project uses fictional practice data for learning purposes. The findings do not represent a real company's marketing performance.
